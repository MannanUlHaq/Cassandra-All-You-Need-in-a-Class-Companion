Cassandra: All You Need in a Class Companion

Cassandra records, organizes, and transforms your lectures into structured notes instantly; helping you learn smarter, revise faster, and stay ahead.

Login as Teacher Login as Student

The Challenge in Modern Classrooms

  • Students struggle to focus during long lectures.
  • Try to listen, understand, and write at the same time.
  • Causes incomplete and messy notes.
  • Important explanations are often missed.
  • Becomes difficult to review properly.
  • Cassandra captures everything automatically.
  • Students can focus fully on learning.
  • Students feel stressed when they have to worry about missing important points.

How Cassandra Transforms Lectures

Cassandra captures your entire lecture automatically and turns it into clear, organized study material. It records what was said, saves important visuals, and brings everything together into easy-to-understand notes. so students can focus on learning instead of writing.

Clear Voice Capture: Records the instructor’s voice clearly, even in busy classrooms.
Automatic Note Creation: Converts lectures into clean, well-structured notes instantly.
Slide & Board Capture: Saves important content from slides and whiteboards automatically.
Everything in One Place: Combines audio, visuals, and notes into a single organized view.
Easy-to-Read Structure: Organizes content clearly so reviewing becomes simple and fast.
Better Understanding: Helps students grasp concepts more clearly with complete lecture context.
